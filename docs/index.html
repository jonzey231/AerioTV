AerioTV Privacy Policy

Applies to AerioTV for iOS, iPadOS, tvOS, macOS, Android, and Android TV.
Effective date: July 8, 2026

The short version. AerioTV does not collect your personal data. There is no AerioTV account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you configure, including your playlists, server addresses, and login credentials, stays on your device and is sent only to the servers you choose. We have no servers of our own that receive your data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the AerioTV applications ("AerioTV," "the app," "we," "us") handle information. AerioTV is an IPTV player that connects to media sources you supply, such as a Dispatcharr server, an Xtream Codes portal, or an M3U playlist with an XMLTV electronic program guide. AerioTV is not affiliated with, and does not provide, any streaming content or subscription. You are responsible for the sources you add.

2. Information you provide

To use AerioTV you enter connection details for your own media sources. Depending on your setup, this may include:

  • Server addresses and URLs (for example a Dispatcharr host, an Xtream Codes portal, or M3U and XMLTV links).
  • Usernames, passwords, or API keys required by those services.
  • App preferences such as favorites, channel groupings, layout choices, and reminders.

This information is stored locally on your device so the app can operate. It is not transmitted to us, because we do not operate a backend that receives it. On Android, stored credentials are encrypted at rest on the device.

3. Data stored on your device

AerioTV keeps the following on the device only:

  • Your source configuration and credentials, as described above.
  • App settings, favorites, and viewing preferences.
  • A local cache of program guide data, channel logos, and artwork to make the app responsive.
  • Recording metadata and any recordings you create, which are saved through your own DVR-capable server or on the device where applicable.

None of this is uploaded to us or to any third party for our benefit.

4. Network connections

AerioTV connects directly from your device to the following, and only as needed to function:

  • Your media sources. The servers and playlists you configure. AerioTV sends the credentials and requests required to authenticate and stream. What those services do with that information is governed by their own operators and policies, not by us.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB). Used to fetch optional movie and show metadata and artwork. Requests include the title or identifier being looked up. See the TMDB privacy policy. AerioTV uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • YouTube. Used only if you choose to play a trailer or related video. This is handled by YouTube and Google. See the Google privacy policy.
  • Channel logo and artwork sources. Public logo catalogs or image URLs referenced by your playlist, fetched to display artwork.

These connections are made straight from your device to those services. AerioTV does not route your traffic through any intermediary owned by us.

5. What AerioTV does not do

  • No AerioTV user accounts and no sign-in to any service we run.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software development kits.
  • No collection of your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. We do not receive your personal information in the first place.

6. Device permissions

AerioTV requests only the permissions needed to play media:

  • Network and internet access to reach the sources you configure.
  • Local network access (on Apple platforms) so the app can connect to a server running on your home network.
  • Notifications to show playback controls and optional reminders you set. You can decline or disable these.
  • Background playback and keep-awake (foreground media service and wake lock on Android; background audio on Apple platforms) so playback continues while you use other apps or the screen is off.

These permissions are used solely to operate the app and are not used to gather information about you.

7. Third-party services

When you use features that rely on TMDB, YouTube and Google, or content from your own providers, those parties handle your requests under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them. AerioTV does not control and is not responsible for the practices of services you connect to.

8. Children's privacy

AerioTV is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app collects no personal information for us to hold.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. You can remove sources, clear the app's cache, or reset the app from within its settings. Uninstalling AerioTV removes the app's locally stored configuration, credentials, and cache from that device. Data held by your own media provider, TMDB, or Google is managed by those services.

10. Security

AerioTV stores your configuration locally and, on Android, encrypts stored credentials at rest. Connections to your sources use whatever transport your server supports. Because AerioTV talks directly to services you choose, keep your source credentials private and prefer secure (HTTPS) endpoints where your provider offers them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. Continued use of AerioTV after an update means you accept the revised policy.
